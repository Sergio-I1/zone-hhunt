## Full description (4000 characters max)
Zone Hunt turns hide-and-seek into a game you play outside with your friends, on a live map.

One person creates a game and everyone else joins by scanning a QR code or typing a 6-letter code. Pick who hides and who seeks, or let the game deal out secret roles. Then the hiders scatter while the seekers wait at base.

THE ZONE CLOSES IN
The play area is a circle on the map that shrinks during the game. Stay inside it, because hiders caught outside get exposed. You set how big it starts, how small it gets and how many times it shrinks.

SIX WAYS TO PLAY
- Classic: seekers hunt, caught hiders are out, and every hider's spot pings on the map every few minutes.
- Infection: every hider who gets caught becomes a seeker.
- Manhunt: everyone is visible the whole time. Pure chase.
- Last Zone Standing: the zone never stops closing.
- Freeze Tag: caught hiders freeze until a teammate runs over and types their unfreeze code.
- Sardines: one hider, and everyone who finds them hides with them.

POWER-UPS
Walk to power-ups on the map to grab them: an extra life, a jammer that blacks out the seekers' maps, a ghost mode that hides you from pings, a rat that exposes another hider, a decoy ping, and a disguise that makes any hider look like someone else, even a seeker.

MORE
- Lives and escape time, so one tag doesn't have to end your game
- Teams of hiders with their own colors, names and icons
- Take a photo to use as your icon on the map
- Results at the end: who lasted longest, where everyone was caught, and a time-lapse replay of the whole game
- Every timing is adjustable

FREE AND FULL
Classic and Manhunt are free. A one-time $2.99 unlock adds Infection, Last Zone Standing, Freeze Tag, Sardines and power-ups. Only the host needs it: everyone in their games plays with the full unlock for free.

Your location is shared only with the players in your game, and messages are encrypted with your game's code. No accounts, no ads.
