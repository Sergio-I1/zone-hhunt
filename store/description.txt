Peekaboom turns hide-and-seek into a game you play outside with your friends, on a live map.

One person creates a game and everyone else joins by scanning a QR code or typing a 6-letter code. No accounts, no sign-up. Pick who hides and who seeks, or let the game deal out secret roles. Then the hiders scatter while the seekers wait.

THE ZONE CLOSES IN
The play area is a circle on the map that shrinks during the game. Stay inside it, because hiders caught outside get exposed.

6 WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: seekers hunt, and every hider's spot pings on the map every few minutes.
• Manhunt: everyone is visible the whole time. Pure chase.
• Infection: every hider who gets caught becomes a seeker.
• Zones: the zone never stops closing.
• Freeze Tag: caught hiders freeze until a teammate runs over and unfreezes them.
• Sardines: one hider, and everyone who finds them hides with them.

POWER-UPS
Walk to power-ups on the map: an extra life, a Jammer that blacks out the seekers' maps, Ghost mode, Superstar, Detour, a Decoy and a Disguise.

AFTER EVERY GAME
Get a funny recap ("You found Ana in 40 seconds… but Bo took you 3 minutes!"), watch the whole chase again on the map, and win a random frame for your icon. Collect all 9.

Your location is shared only with the players in your game, only while you play. No ads, no subscriptions.
