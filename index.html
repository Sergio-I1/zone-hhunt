Version 3 · what's new
  • Power-up limit: a set number are always in play. When one is used, a new one appears.
  • Stacking switch: let hiders hold several power-ups, or only one at a time.
  • Simpler lobby: the main settings up front, the rest under Advanced settings.
  • Caught hiders drop any power-ups they were holding.
  • Animated example of each game mode in the lobby, next to previews of the hider's and seeker's screens.
  • New mode: Sardines. One hider, and everyone who finds them hides too.
  • Results page: a game-over flash with your stats, a table of everyone, a map of where people were caught, and a time-lapse replay.
  • Teams: split hiders into 2 to 4 teams with their own colors, names and icons.
  • Name swap can now target anyone, and there's a new Disguise power-up.
  • Photo icons: take a picture in the lobby and it replaces your dot on the map.
  • Tap a power-up under Advanced settings to watch an example of it.
  • Phone screen examples for tagging, getting tagged and using a power-up.
  • Players whose phone locks or leaves the page show as asleep (💤) at their last spot, and pick back up when they return.
